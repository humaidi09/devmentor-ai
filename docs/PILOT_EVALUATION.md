# Limited campus pilot evaluation plan

## Goal and scope

Determine whether a small group can understand Close AI, find a current campus support source, see its verification state, choose a next step and reach a person. This is usability and safety feedback, not a representative outcome study. Start only after [pre-launch gates](PRE_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md) pass. Use a dedicated pilot environment with verified real records; never treat the fictional demo as campus advice.

Recruit a small voluntary group across Bangla, English and mixed-language users, small and large screens, and varied accessibility needs. Avoid collecting protected characteristics unless a participant independently chooses to disclose an access need. Let users withdraw and give an alternative human contact.

## Tasks

1. Find an academic support resource, read its official source and last-checked date, and explain who to contact.
2. Find technology/financial information, recognize uncertainty or a missing detail, and use the human-support option.
3. Save a resource and explicitly create/edit/delete a next-step checklist.
4. View BFS in Visual Learning, pause it and explain one step using the text alternative.
5. Use CP Coach in manual mode and find a hint without being shown a forced solution.
6. Change language and privacy/notification choices, inspect export and describe the app-data reset versus full account deletion.
7. If optional integrations are enabled, preview and disconnect a Codeforces handle without sharing private account credentials.

## Feedback and measures

Ask: Was the purpose clear? Could you find a verified source and a person? What detail was missing? Was Bangla understandable? Could you use keyboard or screen reader? Did saving or deleting data behave as expected? Did the app feel private? Which step was confusing? Accept structured categories (helpful, incorrect information, technical problem, accessibility issue) and optional short comments only through an approved channel; do not request personal support stories or chat transcripts.

Record task completion, whether source/verification and human handoff were understood, and any critical privacy/accessibility failure. Record counts and anonymized themes rather than surveillance or individual scores. Do not invent impact percentages or claim general effectiveness from a small pilot.

Classify issues as **Critical** (security/privacy or dangerous support advice), **High** (wrong resource, blocked core flow or major access barrier), **Medium** (material confusion), **Low** (minor defect), or **Enhancement**. Stop the affected flow for Critical issues; assign an owner and retest High issues before expansion. Review all findings with the campus support owner and maintainers, decide what to correct, document residual risks and only then consider a wider release.
